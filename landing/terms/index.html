SplitNext is a tally for small groups. It records who paid, who shares a cost, and suggests transfers that would zero the balances. It never moves money. Settling up happens outside the app, between you.

There are no user accounts. Anyone with a live capability token for a group can read and change that group from that device. Keep invite links to the people you mean to include. An invite is one-use and lasts seven days.

Amounts are integer cents. Splits are frozen on the expense so every device shows the same numbers. The app can be wrong, delayed, or unavailable. Do not treat it as a bank, invoice, or legal record of a debt.

The service is provided as-is. We may change or stop it. Store listings are not up yet; the web app is the same product in a browser.

See Privacy for what is stored and how to ask for deletion.
